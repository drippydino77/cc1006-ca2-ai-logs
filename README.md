# AI logs: CC1006 Digital Communication for Impact, CA2 video

The AI usage log for our CA2 video (Topic 4: AI for Education Equity), declared in our AI Declaration Form.

- **Tool:** Claude Code (Anthropic), one session, 17–24 Nov 2026
- **What the AI did:**
  - helped structure the script
  - wrote the Blender and ffmpeg code that builds, animates and edits the video
  - synthesised the music and sound effects
  - ran checks: readability, speech-to-text, loudness
- **Voices:** ElevenLabs
- **What we did:**
  - wrote the HoMA script and the voice lines
  - chose the story, characters, style and call to action
  - gave every round of feedback (ours, our teacher's and our testers')
  - approved each version

## Files
- [`conversation.md`](conversation.md): the readable log, with every message from us and the AI and one line per action the AI took. **Start here.**
- [`raw/transcript.jsonl`](raw/transcript.jsonl): the complete raw session log (one JSON event per line).
- [`raw/subagents/`](raw/subagents/): logs of the three helper agents the AI started during the session.
